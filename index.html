Why this exists

Most gratitude journals are boring. And you need discipline. Gratidude talks to you like a sarcastic drill instructor, so hopefully you keep coming back.

FAQ

Why does it exist?

Gratidude asks for three things you're grateful for, once a day, then gets out of your way. No feed, no affirmations, no confetti. You write three lines and carry on with your morning.

Who built this?

Just one person — not a team, not a startup. No investors, no roadmap meetings. Someone who got fed up with gratitude apps being dull and built a blunter one.

Are my gratitudes private?

Yes. Nobody else can see what you write — no public feed, no sharing. Add your email only if you want it synced and saved for good; it doesn't get any more public than that.

Why is it Gratidude AI? Where is the AI?

There is no AI. But if you want to be successful these days, you've got to put it in the name.
