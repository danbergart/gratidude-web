Build the habit.

Three things a day only does anything if you actually come back. Three ways to make that automatic.

Daily reminder

Daily reminder

One nudge a day, at the time you pick. Not wired up in the beta yet - this remembers your choice for when it is.

Make it your homepage

Set Gratidude as your browser's homepage or new-tab page, and you'll write three things before the internet gets its hooks in.

A page can't set this itself - open your browser settings, find "Homepage" or "On startup", and paste gratidude.ai.

Add it to your phone

Open gratidude.ai in your phone's browser, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". You get a one-tap icon that opens straight to today.

Why this exists.

Gratidude asks for three things you're grateful for, once a day, then gets out of your way. No streak confetti, no affirmations, no feed. You write three lines and carry on with your morning.

From the person who built it

“I kept hearing how important gratitude is. Three things a day, life changing (apparently). But every app and journal I tried were insanely dull. Why can't it be at least mildly entertaining? That would make the habit actually stick. So I built Gratidude.”

Founder, Gratidude

How it works

  1. Open the page and write three things. Thirty seconds, tops.
  2. They're saved for you — no account needed to start.
  3. Come back tomorrow. The journal fills up whether you notice or not.

Make it your homepage and the habit takes care of itself: three things before the internet.
